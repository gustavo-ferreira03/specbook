# Code of conduct

Specbook welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat contributors, users and maintainers with respect in issues, discussions, pull requests and other project spaces.

Discuss the work directly and explain disagreements with evidence. Give people room to ask questions or correct a mistake. Criticism of an idea is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of someone's private information are not.

Keep reports and examples free of credentials and personal data. Do not use project spaces to target someone, organize harassment or continue contact after they have asked you to stop.

## Reporting and enforcement

Gus is responsible for conduct reports and moderation. Use an available private contact method on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/gustavo-ferreira03). If none is listed, request a private contact channel in an issue without naming the people involved or describing sensitive events publicly. Report software vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Describe what happened and provide relevant context if you can do so safely. Gus will limit sharing to what is needed to investigate and respond. This is not a promise of absolute confidentiality, particularly where safety or legal obligations require disclosure.

Moderation may include editing or removing content, a warning, or a temporary or permanent restriction on participation, depending on the conduct and its impact. Gus will explain a moderation decision when doing so does not expose private information; people affected can ask for reconsideration through the same private channel.
