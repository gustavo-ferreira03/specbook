# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and small teams who build web applications and want their behaviour documented and verified. They talk to Specbook's agent, or let their own coding agent request tests over MCP, then come back to review Specs, runs and failures. Sessions are short and focused: check what broke, read the evidence, approve a change, move on.

## Product Purpose

Specbook turns conversations about a web application into readable, executable Specs with verification status, run history and evidence. Success is opening Specbook and knowing within seconds what the application should do and whether it still does.

## Brand Personality

Precise, authorial, trustworthy. Monochrome and high contrast with a strong typographic signature, in the spirit of Vercel and Geist. Elegance comes from typography and rigour, not from colour or decoration.

## Anti-references

- The generic shadcn template: neutral grays, white cards, the default black button, nothing that says Specbook.
- A terminal or test-runner look: hacker dark mode, code in the foreground.
- Colourful or playful: gradients, illustrations, saturated colour everywhere.
- Decorative metrics and grids of identical cards.

## Design Principles

- The Spec is the centre. `spec.ts` is an escape hatch, collapsed by default.
- State and failure are visible where the work happens. Status is always icon plus text, never colour alone.
- Typography carries hierarchy and identity.
- The path from conversation to evidence stays short.
- Core actions and context survive narrow screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA in light and dark themes, visible keyboard focus, descriptive labels, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Body text is 14px and nothing drops below 12px.
